About

I received my Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the University of Florida, where my dissertation focused on networked systems and multimodal machine learning for decentralized datasets, advised by Dr. Mostafa Reisi and Dr. Aleksandr Kazachkov.

My work sits between mathematical optimization and machine learning: cutting planes and disjunctive cuts for mixed-integer programs, and distributed optimization methods for data that cannot be pooled in one place.
